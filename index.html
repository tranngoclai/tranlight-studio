Tranlight Studio

A personal video production tool.

Tranlight Studio is a small, non-commercial tool used by its owner to produce short educational videos and upload them to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts.

How it works

  1. Videos are rendered locally from scripts written by the owner.
  2. The owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and grants the video.upload permission.
  3. The tool uploads a finished video to the owner's TikTok inbox via the Content Posting API.
  4. The owner reviews, adds a caption and publishes the draft manually in the TikTok app.

The tool never posts publicly on its own, never reads other users' data, and is not offered to the public.
